the #1 best-selling digital marketing guide for small business

See You on the Internet is a frustration-free resource for building a website, selling online, search engine optimization, social media, email marketing, and online advertising... that you'll actually enjoy reading.

The proven, frustration-free way to make your business stand out online, from one of North America's leaders on digital marketing for small businesses

Today, you can launch a website, create social media feeds, and get products and services to market on some of the world's most powerful sales platforms in a matter of hours. But marketing your small business effectively takes some careful thought. In See You on the Internet, Avery Swartz, one of North America's top tech leaders, gives you a failsafe framework to plan and execute a brilliant digital marketing strategy with confidence. And you don't need a technical background to follow it. In five simple steps, you will learn to build your brand, increase your customers, and generate more revenue.

about the author

Avery Swartz is the founder and CEO of Camp Tech, the tech workshop company for non-technical people. She was ranked number 5 on Search Engine Journal's Top 50 Women in Marketing list. Avery is the resident tech expert on CTV Your Morning and has contributed to The Globe and Mail, Chatelaine, and Today's Parent. Avery hosts the A.I. Meets World podcast from Microsoft and the Globe and Mail Content Studio, and has been a Professor at both Ryerson University and Humber College. She lives in Toronto.
